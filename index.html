About Me

The Person Behind the Code

🧑‍💻

Hello! I'm Pavle Mijatović

I'm a Staff/Principal iOS Engineer based in Belgrade, Serbia. With over 18 years of experience in mobile development, I've had the privilege of building apps that millions of people use daily.

My journey started with Objective-C back when the App Store was young, and I've evolved alongside iOS—from UIKit to SwiftUI, from MVC to modern architectures. I specialize in complex domains: banking apps that handle sensitive transactions, crypto wallets that secure digital assets, and retail apps that connect the physical and digital worlds through BLE technology.

Beyond coding, I'm passionate about clean architecture, mentoring fellow developers, and staying at the cutting edge of Apple's ecosystem. When I'm not building apps, you'll find me exploring new technologies or contributing to open-source projects.
