About Me

The Person Behind the Code

Hello! I'm Pavle Mijatović

I'm a Principal iOS Engineer based in Belgrade, Serbia, with over 20 years of experience in software and mobile development. I hold an MSc in Electrical Engineering & Computer Science from the University of Belgrade.

My expertise spans the full spectrum of iOS development—from modernizing legacy Objective-C codebases to building greenfield apps with SwiftUI. I specialize in complex domains: banking applications with secure transactions, cryptocurrency wallets with hardware integration, and retail apps leveraging Bluetooth Low Energy technology.

Beyond coding, I'm passionate about implementing secure systems, driving CI/CD automation, and mentoring engineering teams. I bring both technical depth and leadership experience to every project, having led teams of up to 10 developers across companies like Chess.com, Clarivate, Ebury Partners, and Tiqets.

When I'm not building apps, you'll find me reading (sci-fi, history, politics), playing chess, or staying fit at the gym.
